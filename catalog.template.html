The Kit.

Free models for your world, in the house style: soft, cel-shaded Smooth Voxels. Copy a tag into your page and it's there. Each one is a few lines of plain text, so you (or your AI agent) can open it and change the colors or the shape. All CC0.

Tags are positioned to stand on flat ground. Models are centered on their transform and every voxel is 12.5 cm, so to resize one, change scale3d and raise it by half its new height.
